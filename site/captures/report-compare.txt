Before/after comparison around a change date

Usage: auditmyvisitors report compare [OPTIONS] --since <SINCE>

Options:
      --url <URL>        The page URL (or omit for full-site comparison)
      --before <BEFORE>  Days before the change date to include [default: 30]
      --after <AFTER>    Days after the change date to include [default: 30]
      --since <SINCE>    The change date (YYYY-MM-DD), e.g. a deploy date
      --verbose          Enable verbose logging
  -h, --help             Print help
