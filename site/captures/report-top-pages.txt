Top pages ranked by sessions

Usage: auditmyvisitors report top-pages [OPTIONS]

Options:
  -d, --days <DAYS>        Number of days to look back (default: 28)
  -n, --limit <LIMIT>      How many pages to show (default: 20)
      --sort-by <SORT_BY>  Sort by: sessions (default), clicks, impressions [default: sessions]
      --verbose            Enable verbose logging
  -h, --help               Print help
